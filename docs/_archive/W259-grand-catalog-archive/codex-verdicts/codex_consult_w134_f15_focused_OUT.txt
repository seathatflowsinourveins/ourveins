46:# 📑 Introduction to PageIndex
48:Are you frustrated with vector database retrieval accuracy for long professional documents? Traditional vector-based RAG relies on semantic *similarity* rather than true *relevance*. But **similarity ≠ relevance** — what we truly need in retrieval is **relevance**, and that requires **reasoning**. When working with professional documents that demand domain expertise and multi-step reasoning, similarity search often falls short.
50:Inspired by AlphaGo, we propose **[PageIndex](https://vectify.ai/pageindex)** — a **vectorless**, **reasoning-based RAG** system that builds a **hierarchical tree index** from long documents and uses LLMs to **reason** *over that index* for **agentic, context-aware retrieval**.
51:It simulates how *human experts* navigate and extract knowledge from complex documents through *tree search*, enabling LLMs to *think* and *reason* their way to the most relevant document sections. PageIndex performs retrieval in two steps:
53:1. Generate a “Table-of-Contents” **tree structure index** of documents
54:2. Perform reasoning-based retrieval through **tree search**
57:  <a href="https://pageindex.ai/blog/pageindex-intro" target="_blank" title="The PageIndex Framework">
64:Compared to traditional vector-based RAG, **PageIndex** features:

69:- **Human-like Retrieval**: Simulates how human experts navigate and extract knowledge from complex documents.

46:# 📑 Introduction to PageIndex
47:
48:Are you frustrated with vector database retrieval accuracy for long professional documents? Traditional vector-based RAG relies on semantic *similarity* rather than true *relevance*. But **similarity ≠ relevance** — what we truly need in retrieval is **relevance**, and that requires **reasoning**. When working with professional documents that demand domain expertise and multi-step reasoning, similarity search often falls short.
49:
50:Inspired by AlphaGo, we propose **[PageIndex](https://vectify.ai/pageindex)** — a **vectorless**, **reasoning-based RAG** system that builds a **hierarchical tree index** from long documents and uses LLMs to **reason** *over that index* for **agentic, context-aware retrieval**.
51:It simulates how *human experts* navigate and extract knowledge from complex documents through *tree search*, enabling LLMs to *think* and *reason* their way to the most relevant document sections. PageIndex performs retrieval in two steps:
52:
53:1. Generate a “Table-of-Contents” **tree structure index** of documents
54:2. Perform reasoning-based retrieval through **tree search**
55:

57:  <a href="https://pageindex.ai/blog/pageindex-intro" target="_blank" title="The PageIndex Framework">

62:### 🎯 Core Features
63:
64:Compared to traditional vector-based RAG, **PageIndex** features:
65:- **No Vector DB**: Uses document structure and LLM reasoning for retrieval, instead of vector similarity search.
66:- **No Chunking**: Documents are organized into natural sections, not artificial chunks.
67:- **Better Explainability and Traceability**: Retrieval is based on reasoning, traceable and interpretable, with page and section references. No more opaque, approximate vector search (“vibe retrieval”).
68:- **Context-Aware Retrieval**: Retrieval depends on your full context (e.g., conversation history and domain knowledge), and easily incorporates new context.
69:- **Human-like Retrieval**: Simulates how human experts navigate and extract knowledge from complex documents.
70:
71:PageIndex powers a reasoning-based RAG system that achieved **state-of-the-art** [98.7% accuracy](https://github.com/VectifyAI/Mafin2.5-FinanceBench) on FinanceBench, demonstrating superior performance over vector-based RAG solutions in professional document analysis. See our [blog post](https://vectify.ai/blog/Mafin2.5) for details.
