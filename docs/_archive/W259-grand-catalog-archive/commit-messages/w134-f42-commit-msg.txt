### #2 Atomicity + Boundary-precedence + Storage-class-immutability — class-boundary precision discipline

Codex T1 caught semantic ambiguity at class boundaries:
- A single observation may span multiple classes (fact + episode + procedural
  insight) → §Atomicity rule mandates pre-classification split
- Same conceptual content may resolve different classes in different contexts
  → §Boundary precedence codifies tie-breaker (each atomic record gets own class)
- Promotion is NOT reclassification → §Storage-class-immutability principle
  prevents historical-record-rewrite anti-pattern

These 3 wording refinements upgrade the taxonomy from "category labels" to
"epistemically-disciplined record-classification system" with explicit
record-immutability invariant.

### #3 Evidence-synthesis ladder replaces reclassification ladder — fundamental refinement

The most LOAD-BEARING codex T1 catch: prior framing "RUNBOOK → SEMANTIC →
PROCEDURAL" implied records mutate class. Refined framing "evidence

PROCEDURAL record with backlinks" preserves source-record-immutability while
enabling synthesis. The ladder is **evidence at the ARC level**, not
mutation at the RECORD level.
