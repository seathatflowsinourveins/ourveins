- W265 estimates "6 services NSSM/ad-hoc -> Servy" and "~3 h end-to-end" with 15/15/30/30/30/60 minute steps (`W265-service-management-sota-2026-05-17.md:74,80-88`). - W265 does not cite any parity check for NSSM-specific failure controls such as Throttle, RestartDelay, or AppExit granularity; those strings are absent from the reviewed W265 docs by `Select-String` evidence. The Servy benefit claims cite health-check/auto-recovery/log rotation/env expansion (`W265-service-management-sota-2026-05-17.md:13,87,94-97`), but not NSSM feature-equivalence.
