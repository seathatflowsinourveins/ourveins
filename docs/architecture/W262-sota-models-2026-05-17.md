## §4 What's MISSING

1. **Small-fast GPU reranker** — fixed by Job #4 (Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B on `:8082`).
2. **Vision / multimodal** — fixed by Job #7 (Qwen3-VL-8B swap target). Current `:8080` "multimodal" claim is unsupported by the loaded model.
3. **Speech-to-text (ASR)** — absent. SOTA 2026-05: **Parakeet-TDT-1.1B** (1.8% WER, ~2000× RT, ~3–4 GiB) for latency; **Canary-Qwen-2.5B** (5.63% WER, #1 on Open-ASR) for accuracy [^cs-asr][^nf-asr]. Whisper-large-v3-turbo (~6 GiB) is the multilingual fallback. **ADD when a voice workload exists** — none today.
4. **GLiNER / dedicated NER** — premature; Qwen3.5-4B (Job #5) handles this.
5. **Vision embedder** — Qwen3-VL-Embedding in flight on llama.cpp [^ggml-19516]; defer.

Adopt **now** (all VRAM-fit on 24 GiB; evidence-converged): #3, #4, #5, #8. Adopt **next** as swap target: #7. **Hold**: #1, #2, #6. **Defer**: FT, ASR.
